Mark-VII Interface System

A Stark-inspired theme for Comparinator: classic Mark III/XLII red & gold armor, rendered as a clean, legible product UI with restrained HUD accents — not a costume. Supports both a light ("powered down") and dark ("suit up") mode. Toggle the switch above to preview both.

Gold is the brand

Gold/brass is the only color used for buttons, links, focus rings, and active/checked states. If it's clickable, it's gold.

Red means "different"

Red is reserved exclusively for diff/error semantics — the "different" highlight, destructive actions, failed status. Never decorative.

Green means "match"

Green is reserved exclusively for match/success semantics. Together, red and green stay a dependable, unambiguous pair.

10 — Principles

Motion

Motion is functional, not decorative. Hover the buttons above to feel the timing live.

Hover liftInteractive elements rise 1px and brighten slightly, 160ms ease. No bounce, no overshoot.
Focus glowA 3px gold ring appears instantly on keyboard focus (no fade-in) — clarity over polish for accessibility-critical feedback.
Panel entranceNew results fade + rise 6px over 200ms when a comparison completes.
Arc-reactor pulseThe one ambient animation in the system: a slow 2.6s glow breathe, used only on the decorative reactor badge.
Reduced motionAll transitions and the reactor pulse collapse to near-zero duration under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.
Theme switchBackground and color transitions run at 280ms ease when toggling light/dark — slow enough to read as a mode change, fast enough not to feel laggy.
11 — Principles

Usage Rules

Do Use gold for every clickable/actionable affordance — buttons, links, focus rings, checked toggles.
Don't Use red or green for anything that isn't a diff/status result. No red banners, no green decoration.
Do Keep HUD flourishes (corner brackets, glow) on primary containers only — one layer, not nested.
Don't Let a HUD flourish reduce text contrast. Legibility of the diff results always wins over theming.
Do Preserve all existing keyboard/ARIA behavior from Comparinator — the reskin changes tokens, not structure or interaction.
Don't Introduce the arc-reactor cyan anywhere outside its one decorative badge use.
