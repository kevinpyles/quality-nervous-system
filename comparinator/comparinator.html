How the metrics work:

Similarity measures shared ordered content across both texts. Accuracy treats Text A as the expected reference and measures how faithfully Text B reproduces it. Word order matters. When “Ignore punctuation” is enabled, punctuation remains visible but does not count as a difference.
Comparinator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
